About the model

Trained on real digital-wellness data

AI Mind runs a regression model served by FastAPI, trained on demographic and behavioral data — screen time, sleep, study load, physical activity, and stress — to estimate a composite mental wellness score from 1–10. It's a lightweight lifestyle mirror, not a clinical diagnosis.
